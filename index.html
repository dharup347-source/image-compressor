Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image?

Select one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images, choose your preferred quality, and tap Compress Images.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image compression happens directly in your browser, so your images are not uploaded to a server.

Which image formats are supported?

ImageCompress supports JPG, PNG and WebP images.

Can I compress multiple images?

Yes. You can select multiple images and compress them together.
